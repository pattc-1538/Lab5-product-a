# Glossary

> Collected retroactively: note terms as they come up during the Block O–C interviews, then confirm their
> definitions together in Block D. Never open with "tell me your terminology".

## 1. Terms

| Term | Reading / abbreviation | Definition | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 産地市場 | さんちしじょう | A market near the fishing grounds that receives catch directly from boats (as opposed to a large consumption market such as Toyosu) | |
| 卸売市場法 | おろしうりしじょうほう | The Wholesale Market Act; its 2020 revision lets each market issue its own 業務規程 | |
| 業務規程 | ぎょうむきてい | This market's own operating rules; the basis for trading permissions, fees and payment deadlines | |
| 卸売業者 | おろしうりぎょうしゃ | The party that receives consigned catch from shippers and organizes its sale | |
| 仲卸 | なかおろし | A party licensed by the market that buys from the 卸売業者 and resells | |
| 売買参加者 | ばいばいさんかしゃ | A party permitted by the market to buy directly from the 卸売業者, but not to resell within the market | |
| 買出人 | かいだしにん | A retail buyer at the market | |
| 出荷者 | しゅっかしゃ | A fisher or 漁協 that consigns catch to the market for sale | |
| 上場 | じょうじょう | Putting a lot on the floor for sale | |
| せり | — | A public auction session where the highest bidder buys | |
| 相対取引 | あいたいとりひき | A trade whose price is agreed directly between the two parties | Accounts for most of the real transaction value |
| 仕切 | しきり | Settlement paid to the shipper: sales amount minus fees | |
| 買受代金 | かいうけだいきん | The amount a buyer owes for goods bought | |
| 完納奨励金 | かんのうしょうれいきん | An incentive paid to buyers who pay their 買受代金 in full by the deadline | |
| 2 permission tiers | — | The two legal levels of trading permission among the four participant types | Mapping of types to tiers follows the 業務規程 |
